Kitchen Remodeling

Kitchen Layout Planning: Islands, Appliances & Utility Moves

A kitchen layout should work when the refrigerator, dishwasher and drawers are open—not just when everything is closed for a rendering. Before ordering cabinets, test the room against the way your household cooks, cleans, stores food and passes through.

Updated October 24, 2026 · ADELIE Construction · Sources below

  • Choose appliance models before releasing cabinet drawings
  • Treat the island as a small construction project

Map the daily conflicts first

List three specific problems with the current kitchen: perhaps two people cannot pass at the sink, the dishwasher blocks the trash pullout, or grocery storage is scattered. Draw the room with door swings, windows and the route to adjoining spaces. Use that drawing to evaluate solutions instead of starting with a preferred island size.

Mark a proposed island on the floor and simulate occupied seating and open appliances. A walkway that looks generous when empty may be the busiest route to the backyard. Compare an island with a peninsula or a better perimeter layout if the room is tight.

Choose appliance models before releasing cabinet drawings

Obtain the current installation sheets for the actual models, including cutout dimensions, ventilation provisions and service access. “Standard refrigerator” is not a complete specification. Door swing, handle projection and the room needed to remove drawers can affect a nearby wall or island.

Check the dishwasher door against opposite drawers and passage space. Coordinate sink, faucet and disposal choices with the sink cabinet. If a model changes after approval, have the cabinet and trade drawings checked again before treating it as a harmless substitution.

Treat the island as a small construction project

An island can contain storage, seating, appliances, a sink and electrical service. Each function changes what must fit below the counter and where people stand. Resolve support for the selected counter, end-panel thickness, seating arrangements and appliance access before fabrication.

If the island contains a sink, ask how drainage and venting will be arranged. If it contains cooking equipment, establish the exhaust route and equipment requirements. Do not make the finished island footprint the only drawing the trades receive.

Compare keeping utilities with moving them

Ask for a layout retaining the main utility locations alongside the preferred relocated version. Identify the work each option needs in the floor, walls and adjoining finishes. The additional cost may be worthwhile, but it should buy a clear improvement in daily use.

For a City of San Diego project, City of San Diego: No-Plan Building Permit (IB 203) describes eligibility conditions for certain remodel scopes. It is not a general permit exemption. Have the project team confirm the application route for the actual wall, plumbing and electrical changes. Other local jurisdictions use their own procedures.

Approve one coordinated package

  • Cabinet dimensions, fillers and finished end panels.
  • Appliance model numbers and installation sheets.
  • Sink, faucet, drain and shutoff locations.
  • Lighting, controls and electrical locations reviewed by the responsible professional.
  • Countertop dimensions, seams, support and delivery route.
  • A decision deadline and change process before fabrication.

Keep one current drawing set with a revision date. When an appliance, opening or cabinet changes, identify every affected sheet and supplier rather than relying on a verbal update.

Questions homeowners ask

Is adding an island always an improvement?

No. It can reduce circulation or create collisions between seating and appliances. Judge the layout by usable storage and comfortable movement with the kitchen in use.

Can I order cabinets before the appliance selection is final?

That introduces avoidable risk. If you must proceed, have the cabinet supplier document exactly which dimensions and assumptions are being accepted, and who bears the cost if the final appliance does not fit.
