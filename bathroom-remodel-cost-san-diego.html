San Diego Bathroom Cost Guide

Bathroom Remodel Cost in San Diego: A Scope-Based Guide

ADELIE homeowner guideUpdated October 2026

Quick answer

Swell Contracting’s 2026 San Diego planning guide places its bathroom projects around $25,000–$70,000+, while a limited cosmetic update may be different. That is one contractor’s published range, not a quote or industry average. Scope varies because some prices omit demolition, waterproofing, plumbing, glass, cabinetry or project management.

Planning guidance for San Diego County homeowners. Costs are illustrative, not bids; verify property-specific scope, prices and permit requirements.

Bathrooms are small but construction-dense. Waterproofing, tile labor, plumbing, ventilation, electrical work and glass can make cost per square foot look high. Define the room and assembly details before comparing bids.

A practical way to look at it

When reviewing a bathroom proposal, ask the contractor to describe the shower before the tile goes on. The drain, slope, waterproofing transitions and test procedure tell you more about durability than the tile price.

Guest bath, primary bath and wet room

A compact hall bath with the same layout is different from a primary bath with a curbless shower, freestanding tub and custom vanity. Wet rooms, steam showers and large-format stone introduce additional planning and installation requirements.

Waterproofing is not a finish upgrade

The assembly behind the tile controls durability. Proposals should identify the shower system, drain, slope, transitions, penetrations, testing and substrate preparation—not simply list “tile shower.”

Layout changes affect several trades

Moving a toilet or shower can affect drainage, venting, slab or framing work, waterproofing, electrical and finish repair. Keeping fixtures near existing locations often protects the budget.

Selections must be coordinated

Verify rough-in dimensions, valve components, drain type, glass thickness, tile quantity, trim profiles and lead times. A fixture price online may omit required valves or installation parts.

Permit planning

Plumbing, electrical, mechanical and structural changes may require permits. Confirm jurisdiction-specific requirements before work begins and keep required work visible for inspection.

Create a comparable scope

List demolition, protection, waterproofing, tile areas, fixtures, vanity, countertop, glass, lighting, fan, paint and accessories. ADELIE uses these details to distinguish a complete bath from an attractive but incomplete allowance.

Sources and methodology

This guide separates official requirements from planning estimates. Government sources support permit and contractor statements; cost figures are broad market observations, not promises or project quotes. A dependable number still requires a site review and written scope.

Common Questions

What homeowners ask next

Can a bathroom be remodeled for $20K?

A small, straightforward bath may fit, but full-service San Diego projects with extensive tile or plumbing work often exceed that amount.

Why is shower tile expensive?

The price includes substrate preparation, waterproofing, layout, cutting, setting, grouting and transitions—not only tile.

Should I keep the layout?

Keeping plumbing locations can reduce cost and risk, though the best decision depends on function and existing conditions.
