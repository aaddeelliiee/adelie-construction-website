Fallbrook Kitchen Remodeling

Fallbrook kitchen remodeling with one coordinated general contractor.

Plan the layout, cabinetry, utilities, finishes and construction under a clear scope built around your home.

ADELIE Construction

Plan the room before construction begins.

A successful kitchen remodel connects layout, storage, lighting, ventilation, appliance requirements and finish details. ADELIE Construction coordinates those decisions as one remodeling scope instead of leaving the homeowner to reconcile separate trades.

Before work begins, we document what is included, what still requires a selection and which existing conditions may affect cost or schedule. That preparation helps identify conflicts while plans and specifications can still be adjusted.

ADELIE is a California-licensed general contractor serving Fallbrook, including Downtown Fallbrook, Gird Valley, Winterwarm and Live Oak Park, subject to project fit, site access and scheduling. California contractor license #1157923.

Pre-Construction Checkpoints

Decisions that protect the budget and schedule.

Existing conditions

Review walls, floor levels, visible utilities, access and adjoining finishes before defining the final scope. Concealed conditions are handled through the written project process if discovered.

Appliance specifications

Confirm model numbers and installation requirements before cabinet openings, electrical loads, plumbing locations and ventilation are finalized.

Cabinet and lighting plans

Coordinate elevations, fillers, panels, outlets, switching and task lighting so the finished room works as one system.

Selections and lead times

Set decision deadlines for cabinets, counters, tile, fixtures, hardware and appliances before late selections affect sequencing.

Kitchen Remodeling Scope

Details that shape the finished project.

Layout and workflow

Evaluate work zones, circulation and opportunities to improve the relationship between cooking, storage and gathering areas.

Cabinetry and storage

Coordinate cabinet dimensions, appliance openings, fillers, panels, hardware and functional accessories.

Countertops and tile

Plan fabrication, seams, edges, outlets, backsplashes and transitions before installation.

Lighting and utilities

Coordinate electrical, plumbing, ventilation and appliance requirements with the finish design.

Fallbrook Kitchen Remodeling FAQ

Questions to answer before requesting proposals.

What should be decided before a kitchen remodel starts?

Document the layout, appliance specifications, cabinet plan, plumbing and electrical locations, ventilation, finish selections, scope assumptions and decision deadlines whenever possible.

Can ADELIE coordinate structural, electrical and plumbing work?

Yes. ADELIE plans the kitchen as a coordinated general-contracting scope. Final requirements depend on the existing home, approved design, permits and project agreement.

Do you remodel kitchens throughout Fallbrook?

ADELIE serves Fallbrook and nearby areas subject to project scope, site access and current scheduling.
