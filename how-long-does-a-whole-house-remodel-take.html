How Long Does a Whole-House Remodel Take?

ADELIE homeowner guideUpdated October 2026

Quick answer

A focused room remodel may take several weeks of construction, while a coordinated whole-home renovation can take many months. The full project timeline starts before demolition: design, engineering, permits, selections and long-lead ordering may equal or exceed the time spent building.

Planning guidance for San Diego County homeowners. Costs are illustrative, not bids; verify property-specific scope, prices and permit requirements.

A construction duration is not the same as a project duration. Homeowners should plan from the first scope decision through final inspection and closeout, including time for revisions and product procurement.

A practical way to look at it

The schedule should show decisions as well as construction. If cabinets must be approved in March to arrive before installation in June, that approval date is a real project milestone—not an administrative detail.

Preconstruction comes first

Feasibility, design, budgeting, engineering, permit documents, selections and contracting establish whether the construction schedule is achievable. Starting demolition with unresolved decisions transfers planning time into costly downtime.

Typical sequence

Protection and demolition lead to framing and rough plumbing, electrical and mechanical work. Required inspections occur before insulation and drywall. Cabinets, tile, trim, paint, fixtures, final inspections and punch work follow in a coordinated order.

What commonly causes delays

Late selections, revised design, concealed conditions, permit corrections, failed inspections, unavailable products, owner changes and trade dependencies are common causes. Weather and site access also matter for additions or exterior work.

How to make the schedule credible

Ask for decision deadlines, procurement status, inspection assumptions and the critical path—not only a start and finish date. Confirm whether the schedule includes weekends, holidays, cure times and punch work.

Living arrangements

The number of affected bathrooms, kitchen downtime, dust control, pets, remote work and utility interruptions should shape the plan. Temporary housing may be appropriate even when construction is shorter than a whole-home project.

ADELIE scheduling approach

ADELIE connects selections, ordering, trade sequencing and homeowner decisions to milestones. A schedule is most useful when its assumptions and dependencies are visible.

Sources and methodology

This guide separates official requirements from planning estimates. Government sources support permit and contractor statements; cost figures are broad market observations, not promises or project quotes. A dependable number still requires a site review and written scope.

Common Questions

What homeowners ask next

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Construction often spans several weeks, but cabinets, permits and design can make total project planning much longer.

Do permits always delay a project?

They add a required planning step; duration varies by jurisdiction, scope and document quality.

Can work start before every selection is made?

Sometimes, but products affecting rough work, dimensions or lead times should be finalized early.
