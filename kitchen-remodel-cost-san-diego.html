San Diego Kitchen Cost Guide

Kitchen Remodel Cost in San Diego: What Drives the Price?

ADELIE homeowner guideUpdated October 2026

Quick answer

Published San Diego contractor guides commonly place full kitchen remodels somewhere between roughly $35,000 and $130,000+, with cosmetic work below that and custom, structural or luxury projects above it. The most important variables are cabinetry, layout changes, appliances, electrical and plumbing work, and exactly what the contract includes.

Planning guidance for San Diego County homeowners. Costs are illustrative, not bids; verify property-specific scope, prices and permit requirements.

A kitchen is dense with cabinetry, utilities, fixtures and finish decisions, so its cost is not well explained by square footage alone. A smaller kitchen with custom cabinets and relocated plumbing may cost more than a larger kitchen that keeps its footprint.

A practical way to look at it

Cabinet drawings and appliance model numbers resolve more budget questions than a mood board. They expose panel sizes, clearances, electrical loads, ventilation and the filler pieces that are easy to miss in an early estimate.

Use scope tiers, not one average

A refresh may retain cabinet boxes and layout. A full remodel usually replaces cabinets, counters, flooring, lighting and fixtures. A custom or structural remodel may move walls, windows, plumbing and ventilation. Be clear which tier you mean before comparing numbers.

Cabinetry is a major lever

Stock, semi-custom and custom cabinets differ in sizing, construction, finish, accessories and installation requirements. Confirm whether the quoted number includes demolition, design, delivery, fillers, panels, crown, hardware and installation.

Layout and systems change the math

Moving a sink, range or refrigerator can affect plumbing, electrical, gas, ventilation, framing and finishes. Panel capacity, required circuits and hood duct routes should be evaluated before the design is treated as final.

Allowances need product-level detail

An allowance should identify quantity, quality, tax, delivery and installation. Appliances and decorative fixtures can change the total quickly, so separate owner purchases from the construction contract.

Permits and inspections

Electrical, plumbing, mechanical or structural work may require permits and inspections. Confirm the requirements with the applicable city or county and identify who obtains permits in the written contract.

Build an estimate-ready scope

Document the layout, cabinet level, countertop material, appliance models, flooring limits, lighting plan and finish responsibilities. ADELIE can then connect design choices to construction scope instead of pricing a collection of inspiration images.

Sources and methodology

This guide separates official requirements from planning estimates. Government sources support permit and contractor statements; cost figures are broad market observations, not promises or project quotes. A dependable number still requires a site review and written scope.

Common Questions

What homeowners ask next

Can I remodel a San Diego kitchen for $50K?

Often, with a controlled scope and selections, especially when the footprint stays. The home and inclusions still matter.

What makes a kitchen remodel expensive?

Cabinetry, appliances, structural changes, relocated utilities and premium fabrication are common drivers.

Do I need plans?

Detailed cabinet and electrical plans improve coordination; structural changes may require professional plans and engineering.
