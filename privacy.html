Website Information

Privacy notice.

What we collect through this website, why we use it and how to contact us about your information.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Information you choose to provide

When you submit a contact, consultation, upload or customer-portal form, you may provide your name, contact details, property or project information, messages, documents and photos. Please do not submit sensitive personal information that is not needed to discuss or manage your project.

How the information is used

ADELIE Construction uses submitted information to respond to inquiries, evaluate requested services, prepare for consultations, communicate about projects, operate the customer portal and maintain business records. Website traffic tools may also collect limited technical information—such as browser type, device type and pages visited—when those tools are enabled.

Service providers

The website may use service providers to host pages, deliver forms, store uploads, secure accounts or measure site performance. Those providers process information for the services they supply. Their own terms and privacy practices also apply.

Sharing and sale

ADELIE Construction may share information with providers or project participants when reasonably needed to respond to you or deliver requested services, and when required by law. ADELIE Construction does not sell website inquiry information.

Retention and security

Information is kept only as reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, business records and applicable obligations. No online system can guarantee absolute security, so avoid sending unnecessary confidential information through a public form.

Your choices

You may ask to review, correct or delete information you submitted through the website, subject to records ADELIE Construction may need to keep. You can also contact us before uploading project documents or photos if you want to discuss another delivery method.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email agustin@adelieconstruction.com or call (760) 705-0418.

This notice describes website practices and is not a promise that every feature or third-party service is active at all times. It will be updated when the website’s data practices materially change.
