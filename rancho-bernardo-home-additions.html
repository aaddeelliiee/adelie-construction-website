Rancho Bernardo Addition FAQ

Practical questions homeowners ask first.

What types of additions can ADELIE plan?

Potential scopes include bedrooms, primary suites, home offices, expanded living areas and other attached space. Feasibility depends on the property, zoning, structure, utilities, access and approved design.

Does a home addition require permits?

Home additions generally require plans, review and permits. Exact requirements depend on the property and approved scope and should be confirmed with the responsible design professionals and local authority.

Can an addition match the existing home?

The planning process should address rooflines, exterior materials, windows, floor elevations, interior transitions and building systems so the new space relates intentionally to the existing home.
