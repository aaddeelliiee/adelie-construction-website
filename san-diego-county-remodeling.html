San Diego County Remodeling: Find Your Permit Jurisdiction

A San Diego County address does not identify a single permitting office. Incorporated cities handle their own local processes; the County provides planning and development services for unincorporated areas. Confirm the parcel’s jurisdiction before applying a cost allowance, review schedule or permit checklist from another project.

Discuss Your San Diego County ProjectLocal Planning Resources

Use the parcel, not just the mailing address

The County’s online services include property research, permit records and land-use information. Its Initial Study Research Report is intended for preliminary development and environmental research on unincorporated properties. Use the address and parcel number to establish which authority should answer the project questions.

Local reference: County PDS Online Services.

Fallbrook and Rancho Bernardo follow different routes

Fallbrook’s community planning information is maintained by the County. Rancho Bernardo is a City of San Diego community. They may both appear in a North County service discussion, but the same permit instructions should not be copied onto both pages. The local guides below direct you to the relevant authority.

Local reference: County Fallbrook resources; City planning work program: Rancho Bernardo.

Ask different questions for different sites

For a septic-served property, collect wastewater-system records before an expansion. For a constrained urban lot, document access and utility routes. For a coastal or historic property, ask the local authority about the applicable review. These are questions to investigate, not automatic requirements based on a broad geographic label.

Compare budgets using the same included work

A coastal exterior renovation and an interior kitchen refresh cannot be priced fairly through one countywide square-foot figure. Separate required site or approval work from selectable finishes, then compare the same scope. ADELIE can use the property information and your priorities to prepare a project-specific discussion.

Bring these details to the first project conversation

  • Property address and assessor parcel number
  • Confirmed city or unincorporated County jurisdiction
  • Available permit and site records
  • A work list separating interior, exterior and new floor area

Use this list to prepare for a discussion with ADELIE. Site conditions and the responsible permitting authority determine the final scope.

Does County planning guidance apply inside every city?

No. The County’s unincorporated-area resources do not replace an incorporated city’s permit instructions. Confirm jurisdiction and use that authority’s current guidance.
